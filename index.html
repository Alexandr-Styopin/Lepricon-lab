Our Parties in Ireland

Music-led nights built around great sound, light and atmosphere.

The concept

Our parties put the music and the room first. We pair a carefully chosen DJ line-up with professional sound and lighting, so the night feels like an experience, not just a playlist.

Who it’s for

We design our parties for people who come for the music: dance-floor regulars, music lovers and friends looking for a night out with a bit more character. Brands that want to meet this crowd are welcome to talk to us.

The atmosphere

Clear, powerful sound, lighting that follows the music and a room that feels immersive. People come to dance, discover new music and share a night that feels special.

The Setup

One team for the music, the sound and the light.

We bring our own professional sound and lighting. When our standard setup suits your venue, the same team that plays the music also looks after how the room sounds and feels, so there are fewer separate suppliers to hire and coordinate. Our current setup is designed for indoor events of up to 100 guests, and can be combined with a DJ set for any style of music, from jazz to modern dance.
