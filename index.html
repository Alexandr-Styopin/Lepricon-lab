Meet the DJ

I’m an Open Format DJ based in Ireland, performing nationwide. Each set is tailored to the audience and the client’s preferences — from club nights to corporate events and private parties. You can book me personally, and if needed, I provide a full sound and lighting setup.

The Setup

Professional sound and lighting package for indoor events of up to 100 guests: premium-quality sound with a professional Electro-Voice ETX Sound System, Roland DJ-707M controller, Shure microphone, lighting including two Stairville Moving Head Spot 250s, a UV lamp, strobe, and LED wash lighting (exact quantity to be confirmed).

Any style of music from jazz to modern dance.

Available as a complete DJ + sound + lighting package for corporate events, bars, clubs, and private parties, including Christmas and New Year celebrations.
