About us

Leprechaun Lab is an Irish sound and lighting project founded by an audiovisual specialist with over 20 years of international experience in professional AV installations, live events and technical production across Ukraine, Poland and Russia.

Notable projects include sound system reconstruction at Moscow’s Gorbushka Cultural Centre, corporate events at Winzavod, including an event featuring Cirque du Soleil, AV installations and technical support at Pravda Club for Vladimir Fonarev and DJ Krush, and sound and lighting production at Rai Club, including MUZ-TV events.

Today, Leprechaun Lab brings this expertise to Ireland, providing professional sound, lighting and event solutions.

Collaborate with us

Let’s Make Something Together

Leprechaun Lab is always open to working with people who share our passion for music, sound and live experiences. We’re looking for DJs, musicians, vocalists, live performers, MCs, visual artists and fellow event professionals to create something memorable together.

Who we’d love to hear from

  • DJs and producers: guest sets, back-to-back sessions and joint club nights
  • Musicians, vocalists and live acts: live performances for private and corporate events
  • MCs and hosts: extra energy for the night
  • Visual artists and VJs: visuals and light shows that move with the music
  • Sound and lighting technicians: sharing expertise and joining projects
  • Venues, promoters and event organisers: partnerships and recurring events

What it can look like

Joint events and showcases, shared bookings, studio and production projects, or simply a conversation about what we could build together.
